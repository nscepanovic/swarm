## 2. Once upon a time: the problem

**Na slajdu**
- Have you heard the term "health-safe food"?
- People buy honey because they want something natural and safe.
- 46% of imported honey samples at the EU border: suspected of fraud.

**Nemanja govori**
"Have you heard the term health-safe food? It is food where you can check
where it comes from and what is inside. People buy honey because they want
something natural and safe. But the EU tested imported honey at the border,
and almost half of the samples were suspected to be adulterated. Suspected,
not proven. The point is: when you buy a jar, you do not know what you eat."

**Izvori**
- 46%: EU OLAF / JRC "From the Hives" (2021-2022),
  https://joint-research-centre.ec.europa.eu/jrc-news-and-updates/food-fraud-how-genuine-your-honey-2023-03-23_en
  Ograda: metodologiju je osporio Global Honey Organization. Na slajdu
  obavezno "suspected", nikad "fake".
- NE koristimo "7 in 10 jars are fake" (Belgrade deck) ni ">80% fake"
  (Frontier deck): bez izvora i jace od onoga sto studija kaze.
- "People buy honey because they want something natural and safe": opsta
  tvrdnja bez brojke. USDA/NHB kaze da rast potrosnje vodi percepcija meda
  kao prirodne zamene za secer (vidi slajd 7), ali to nije merenje motiva
  kupaca. Ako zuri pita, to je nas izvor.

## 3. And every day: why today's options fail

**Na slajdu**
- F1 Trust: the label says "pure". You cannot check it.
- F2 Reach: the honest beekeeper sells only to people who know him.
- F3 Proof is for experts: lab tests are for importers, not for one jar.
- F4 The beekeeper sells in bulk. The value stays with the brand.

**Nemanja govori**
"How do people solve this today? They trust the label, but the label is
what the seller says, nobody can check it. Or they buy from a beekeeper they
know. That works, but only for people who live near him. The real proof is a
lab test, and that is for importers and inspectors, not for a family buying
one jar. And the good beekeeper? He sells in bulk to a packer, and the brand
on the jar keeps the value. I know this because I am that beekeeper."

**Izvori**
- Nema brojki na slajdu, namerno. Ovo je opis trzista iz iskustva, ne
  statistika. Ako hocemo brojku za F4 (udeo cene koji ostaje pcelaru), treba
  izvor: `[udeo cene koji dobija pcelar - Nemanja ima podatak ili trazimo izvor]`.

## 5. And because of that: why it is better

**Na slajdu**
- A1 Trust: an origin record for every batch. Beekeeper, region, harvest.
- A2 Reach: beekeepers from many regions, buyers anywhere.
- A3 Proof comes with the jar: scan it, see it.
- A4 The beekeeper sells direct and gets paid in USDC, same day.

**Nemanja govori**
"Point by point. Trust: you do not trust a label, you see a record for that
batch. On our own farm the record also has hive data, temperature and
weight, so you see the colony was working. Reach: the good beekeeper is not
only local any more, he is on one marketplace with others from other
countries. Proof: you do not need a lab, the proof comes with the jar. And
the beekeeper sells direct and gets paid in USDC the same day, in Brazil or
in Europe."

**Izvori**
- Senzori (temperatura, tezina, vlaga) su vec javni (Frontier i Belgrade
  deck); ovde su dokaz, ne novi proizvod.
- A3: `[da li batch ima i lab test - Nemanja potvrdjuje]`. Dok nije
  potvrdjeno, nigde ne pise "lab tested".
- "10x" se ne pise kao brojka: nemamo merenje. Pixar korak 4 je pokriven
  parovima F1-F4 / A1-A4.

## 7. Until finally: the market

**Na slajdu**
- US: 688.6M lb of honey eaten in 2024. EU imports: 363K tonnes (2022).
- Bottom-up: [buyers] x [avg spend per year] x [take rate] = [our market]
- Next: other foods with the same problem. Then B2B trade.
- Agriculture: ~$2.7T. AgriDex proved B2B agri trade on Solana.

**Nemanja govori**
"Start with honey. In 2024 Americans ate almost 700 million pounds of
honey, a record. Europe imported more than 360 thousand tonnes in 2022, and
that imported honey is exactly where the fraud suspicion is. Our math is
simple: buyers, times what they spend, times our fee. After honey we take
the same system to other foods with the same problem. And then B2B: shops
and importers buying in bulk. That is a trillion-dollar market, and AgriDex
already shows it works on Solana."

**Izvori**
- 688.6M lb (2024, record; 2 lb per capita): USDA ERS Sugar and Sweeteners
  Yearbook, via National Honey Board, 2025-07-29,
  https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/us-honey-consumption-hits-all-time-high-according-to-usda-302513560.html
- 363K tonnes EU uvoza, 2022, vrednost preko EUR 1.24B; Evropa pokriva oko
  60% potraznje sopstvenom proizvodnjom: CBI,
  https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/honey/what-demand
- ~$2.7T: The Defiant o AgriDex-u,
  https://thedefiant.io/news/infrastructure/solana-based-agridex-marks-usd9m-in-stablecoin-trades-across-african-markets
- Top-down za proveru, ne na slajdu: honey market 2025 ~USD 10.3B (IMARC,
  https://www.imarcgroup.com/honey-market; druge firme 9.9B-11.6B).
- Bottom-up formula ceka tri broja od Nemanje: `[buyers]` (koga ciljamo

- B2B agri export: Rizoma. No prize.

- Nobody on Colosseum built a marketplace for bee products. We are the first.
- Source: Colosseum Copilot, 5,400+ submissions, 2026-09-22.

**Nemanja govori**
"Others tried agri marketplaces and traceability on Colosseum. None of them
won. They were software with no producers behind them. We are producers,
with our own farm, our own hives and our own money raised.
AgriDex is the serious one. They start from big commodities in Africa. We
start from the product people eat and grow into B2B. They show the model
works."

**Izvori (Colosseum Copilot, 2026-09-22)**
- `munch` (Radar, 2024-09): social marketplace farmer -> consumer.
- `agrichain` (Breakout, 2025-04): agri marketplace + supply chain tracking.
- `agriverse` (Breakout, 2025-04): D2C produce + farmland RWA.
- `laviem` (Radar, 2024-09): food supply chain onchain.
- `farmtrust` (Breakout, 2025-04): eco-labelling i trasiranje.
- `rizoma` (Cypherpunk, 2025-09): kupci -> meksicki proizvodjaci.
- Nadjeno, nije na slajdu: `4groflow` (Breakout, maslinovo ulje + IoT),
  `tanitrack` (Breakout, Indonezija), `mootrack.xyz` (Breakout, goveda LATAM),
  `tru-market` (Cypherpunk, finansiranje agri trgovine),
  `food-supply-chain-tracking-system` (Radar).
- Pobednici u blizini (vazno za zuri, nije na slajdu): `home-harvest`
  (Breakout, 3rd Place DePIN, $15K, mreza kucnih uzgajivaca hrane);
  `cargobill` (Breakout, 1st Place Stablecoins, $25K, C3 akcelerator,
  stablecoin placanja za supply chain). Hrana + DePIN i stablecoin +
  trgovina mogu da pobede; cist "agri marketplace" jos nije.
- Provera "niko ne radi": `winnersOnly` i `acceleratorOnly` pretrage za agri
  marketplace / trasiranje hrane nisu vratile agri marketplace. "Marketplace
  for honey and bee products" vratilo je samo nas (`hivebits`). Zato zvezdica.
- AgriDex: $9M+ (The Defiant, link gore); $5M VC 2024 (CoinDesk,
