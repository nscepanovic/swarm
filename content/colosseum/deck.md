- 46% of imported honey samples at the EU border: suspected of fraud.
Source: NielsenIQ for the National Honey Board, 52 weeks to 2 Nov 2024. EU OLAF/JRC "From the Hives", 2021-2022 (methodology disputed by Global Honey Organization).

**Nemanja govori**

## 3. And every day: why today's options fail

**Na slajdu**
- F1 Trust: the label says "pure". Nobody can check a label.
- F2 Reach: the honest beekeeper sells to people who know him. Nobody else.
- F3 Proof is for experts: lab tests are for importers and inspectors, not for one jar.
- F4 Price: a US beekeeper gets $2.45 a pound from a packer. He gets $7.15 when he sells it himself. Almost 3x. Most of his honey goes to the packer.
Source: USDA NASS Honey, March 2026 (2025 prices). Croatia: EUR 2.5/kg bulk vs EUR 7-8/kg direct (Glas Slavonije, March 2025).

**Nemanja govori**
"How do people solve this today? They trust the label, but the label is
what the seller says, and nobody can check it. Or they buy from a beekeeper
they know. That works, but only if you live near him. The real proof is a
lab test, and that is for importers and inspectors, not for a family buying
one jar. And the beekeeper? In the US he gets 2 dollars 45 a pound when he
sells to a packer. When he sells the same honey himself, he gets 7 dollars
15. Almost three times more. But he cannot reach the buyers, so most of his
honey goes to the packer. In Croatia it is the same: 2 and a half euros a
kilo in bulk, 7 or 8 when he sells it himself. I know this because I am
that beekeeper. [Nemanja: tvoja brojka iz prakse, ako je imas.]"

**Izvori**
- $2.45/lb (zadruga ili paker) naspram $7.15/lb (pcelar sam na malo), 2025,
  sav med, isto istrazivanje: USDA NASS Honey, mart 2026,
  https://esmis.nal.usda.gov/sites/default/release-files/795818/hony0326.txt
  2024 je bilo $2.11 naspram $5.90 (2,8x). NASS "retail" znaci med koji
  pcelar sam prodaje na malo, ne cena na polici (market.md, 4a).
- "Most goes to the packer": iz NASS proseka se izvodi da je pcelar sam na
  malo prodao oko 13% kolicine u 2025 (izvedeno, nije objavljena brojka).
  Na slajdu zato "most", bez procenta.
- Hrvatska: otkup €2,5/kg, direktno €7-8/kg, Glas Slavonije 23.03.2025,
  https://www.glas-slavonije.hr/regija/2025/03/23/kovacic-na-istoku-hrvatske-nemamo-kome-prodati-med-otkupna-je-cijena-samo-2-5-eura-683475/
- **Nemanja potvrdjuje da 2,9x lici na njegovu praksu** (open-questions #6).
  Ako ima svoju brojku (Srbija ili Hrvatska), ona ide u govor, USDA ostaje

## 5. And because of that: why it is better

**Na slajdu**
- A1 Trust: not a label, a record for that batch. On our own farm, hive data too: temperature, humidity, weight.
- A2 Reach: one marketplace. Beekeepers from four regions, buyers anywhere.
- A3 Proof comes with the jar: scan it, see it. No lab, no expert.
- A4 Price: the beekeeper sells at his direct price. We take 5%. He keeps the rest.

**Nemanja govori**
"Point by point. Trust: you do not trust a label, you see a record for that
batch. On our own farm the record also has hive data, temperature,
humidity and weight, so you see the colony was working. Reach: the good
beekeeper is not only local any more. He is on one marketplace with
beekeepers from other countries, and the buyer can be anywhere. Proof: you
do not need a lab. The proof comes with the jar, you scan it. And price:
the beekeeper sells at his direct price, not the packer price. We take 5%.
He keeps the rest. That is the difference between 2 dollars and 7."

## 9. Business model

**Na slajdu**
- We take 5% of every sale. The beekeeper chooses: he pays it, or adds it to his price.
- Etsy: 6.5% plus about 3% payment fees. Amazon Grocery: 8% to 15%. CrowdFarming: 22% on average.
- Later: a fee on every B2B trade.
- The farm is not the business model. The farm is the proof and the first supply.
Source: Etsy fee policy via Craftybase; sell.amazon.com/pricing (24 Sep 2026); CrowdFarming economic transparency page. 5%: HiveBits.

**Nemanja govori**
"We make money like a farmers' market: 5% of every sale. The beekeeper
