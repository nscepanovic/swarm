## 2. Once upon a time: the problem

**Na slajdu**

- 46% of imported honey samples at the EU border: suspected of fraud.
Source: NielsenIQ for the National Honey Board, 52 weeks to 2 Nov 2024. EU OLAF/JRC "From the Hives", 2021-2022 (methodology disputed by Global Honey Organization).

**Nemanja govori**

## 3. And every day: why today's options fail

- F2 Price: a US beekeeper gets $2.45 a pound from a packer. $7.15 when he sells it himself. Almost 3x.
- F3 Money comes last: he pays for bees, hives and a full season of work, and gets paid after the harvest.
- F4 Reach: the honest beekeeper sells to people who know him. Nobody else.
Source: USDA NASS Honey, March 2026 (2025 prices). Croatia: EUR 2.5/kg bulk vs EUR 7-8/kg direct (Glas Slavonije, March 2025).

**Nemanja govori**

money comes last. He pays for the bees, the hives and a full season of

## 7. Until finally: the market

**Na slajdu**
- Same hive, three buyers. One: every next container apiary sells like the first. One container, one sale.
- Two: companies buy a hive with their name, live data and honey with their own label. 3Bee does this in Italy: 500+ brands, 10,000 beekeepers, EUR 5M Series A.
- Three: beekeepers in our network sell their hives on HiveBits. Their honey follows: US households spend $839M a year on honey. Our 5% = $42M, from honey alone.
- Horizon: B2B trade of the same verified goods, the AgriDex model, in a ~$2.7T agriculture market.
Source: FinSMEs, Dec 2022 (3Bee). NielsenIQ for National Honey Board (52 weeks to 2 Nov 2024). The Defiant on AgriDex.

**Nemanja govori**
"How it scales. It is the same product, the hive, sold to three buyers.
First, our next container apiary sells exactly like the first one. One
container, one sale. We do not need to raise money for every farm, we sell
it. Second, companies. A company buys a hive with its name on it, its
team watches the live data, and it gets honey with its own label. 3Bee in
Italy does this with more than 500 brands and 10 thousand beekeepers, and
raised a 5 million euro Series A. Third, beekeepers from our network in Brazil,

from honey, in one country. And at the end, B2B: shops and importers
buying the same verified goods in bulk. AgriDex already does B2B agri

## 9. Business model
