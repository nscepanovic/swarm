# Pitch video - scenario v15 (2026-10-12)

INTERNO. Prati `deck.md` v15 (osam slajdova po Nemanjinom brief-u i
odobrenom rasporedu od 12.10). Izgovaraju se samo dve ograde: senzor ne
sertifikuje kvalitet (slajd 4) i "as far as we found" (slajd 7).

Duzina: **2 do 3 minuta** (colosseum.com/hackathon, hackathon.md). Ovaj
scenario ima 314 reci, to je oko 2:00 do 2:20 sporim tempom (120 do 110 reci u minuti). Ako pri snimanju
predje 3:00, prvo se na slajdu 5 izbacuju poslednje dve recenice, pa na slajdu 4
recenice o senzorima. Vremena po delu ispod
su okvirna.

Tok: ko smo (1) -> sta smo vec uradili (2) -> zasto krecemo od farme (3)
-> sta gradimo sad (4) -> kako je to jedna masina (5) -> trziste i vizija
(6) -> ko drzi koji deo kruga (7) -> tim i sta sledi (8).

Format: Nemanja u kadru, pa slajdovi. Kadrovi sa farme preko glasa samo
ako postoje na Drive-u i zavedeni su u `materials/index.md`. Demo je
poseban video (`demo-video.md`, nije uskladjen sa v11).

Jezik: prosto i direktno. Bez em dash. Bez rokova, iznosa ask-a, prinosa.

---

## 0:00-0:10 Naslovna (slajd 1)

"Hi, I am Nemanja, founder of HiveBits. We build community-owned farms
and we sell what they produce."

## 0:10-0:35 Dokaz (slajd 2)

"We started the farm with one idea: let anyone invest in beekeeping. We
raised 140 thousand dollars through our token, NECTAR, on MetaDAO. 150
people funded 300 smart hives in 27 hours. It is the first tokenized
farm owned by the community."

(Preko glasa: snimci farme, ako su zavedeni.)

## 0:35-0:55 Problem (slajd 3)

"Here is the problem. When a farmer sells through a middleman, he gets
one third of the price. So we remove the middleman. Crypto was made for
this."

## 0:55-1:25 Marketplace (slajd 4)

"So now we build a marketplace. It is built on trust. The question is: Do
you know what you eat? And do you know what you buy? Here you buy only
from farmers you can trust. We sell our products first. Then we bring
farmers we know. After that, new farmers join with our sensors, so you
can see how they work. You can rent a whole hive for one season, or just
buy the product. The rent is paid before the season, so the farmer knows
his harvest is sold. That is why farmers come to us. We take 5 percent
from every sale."

## 1:25-1:55 Krug (slajd 5)

"This is how it works. The community funds a farm. Or a trusted farmer
joins us. The farm makes products. We sell them on the marketplace. Then
the community funds the next farm. And our community is our first buyer."

## 1:55-2:12 Trziste i sirenje (slajd 6)

"We start with honey. The world buys about 10 billion dollars of honey
every year. After honey, we go to milk and other farm products. Same
model, same marketplace."

## 2:12-2:30 Konkurencija (slajd 7)

"Others do one part of this. Some let you adopt a hive. Some sell land.
We are closing the whole loop."

## 2:30-2:45 Tim i sta sledi (slajd 8)

"We are two engineers and beekeepers. We were at Colosseum two times
before. We want the accelerator to help us scale this model and go to
other kinds of farms. Thank you."

Provera: svaka brojka u govoru (140K, 150, 300, 27h, 2.45, 7.15, 5%, 4.4B,
2.6 do 5.2B) ima izvor u `deck.md` na istom slajdu. Rejz je proslo vreme.
Marketplace je "we are building". Krug je opisan kao model, sa jednom
recenicom o tome sta se desilo (prvi korak, nasa farma). Nema rokova,
iznosa ask-a, prinosa, naknade na rejz, prodaje hardvera. "First" se
koristi samo u Nemanjinoj recenici "first tokenized farm owned by the
community".
