# Pitch video - scenario v10 (2026-10-11)

INTERNO. Prati `deck.md` v10 (osam slajdova po drugom brief-u od 11.10).
Ograde iz beleski se ne izgovaraju, osim one o senzoru (slajd 4) i one
o konkurenciji (slajd 5), koje brief trazi i na slajdu i u govoru.

Duzina: **2 do 3 minuta** (colosseum.com/hackathon, hackathon.md). Ovaj
scenario je oko 2:50 normalnim tempom. Ako je preko 3:00, prvo se
skracuje slajd 7 (trziste: izgovoriti samo Teksas), pa slajd 5
(konkurencija: ne nabrajati svih sest, reci "six platforms on the
slide").

Pixar tok: problem (2) -> sta smo vec uradili (6 se najavljuje u 3) ->
resenje u tri stuba (3) -> poverenje i kako raste (4) -> konkurencija kao
dokaz kategorije i gde smo drugaciji (5) -> dokaz traznje (6) -> trziste
i sirenje (7) -> sledeci korak (8).

Format: Nemanja u kadru, pa slajdovi. Kadrovi sa farme preko glasa samo
ako postoje na Drive-u i zavedeni su u `materials/index.md`. Demo je
poseban video (`demo-video.md`).

Jezik: prosto i direktno. Bez em dash. Bez rokova, iznosa ask-a, prinosa.

---

## 0:00-0:15 Cover (slajd 1)

"Hi, I am Nemanja. We are HiveBits.
HiveBits connects people directly with real farms. You can take part in
production, reserve what a farm will make, or buy directly from a farmer
you can trust.
We started with bees. The model is built for other farms too."

## 0:15-0:40 Problem (slajd 2)

"Between a farm and a customer there are usually a few companies. The
farmer sees only the first one, and that one sets his price.
In the US, a beekeeper gets two dollars forty five a pound from a
packer. If he sells it himself, seven fifteen. Same honey.
People who fund farms, people who buy, and the farmer are in three
different worlds. We want to put them in one place."

## 0:40-1:10 Tri stuba (slajd 3)

"Three ways.
One, you help fund real production. We did this once, with our own
farm: 300 smart hives.
Two, you rent a hive or a cow for a season, or reserve part of a future
harvest. The farmer plans better and you know where your food comes
from. This part is planned.
Three, you buy honey and other products directly from farmers we know.
We are building this now. We take 5 percent of each sale.
These are three different deals with different terms. We do not mix
them."

## 1:10-1:30 Poverenje i mreza (slajd 4)

"Trust comes from people first. We start with beekeepers we know and
work with. Our farm is the first supplier.
We already have hardware under our hives. It measures weight,
temperature and humidity. Later it gives other beekeepers a way to join
and show their production.
To be clear: the sensor shows what happens in the hive. It does not
prove the honey is good. The beekeeper does."

## 1:30-1:55 Konkurencija (slajd 5)

"We are not the first to connect buyers and farmers. Pollenity does
smart hives and hive adoption. CrowdFarming lets you adopt a tree and
get the harvest. Drenio sells local food direct. AgriDex trades
commodities between businesses on Solana. AcreTrader and FarmTogether
sell farmland investments.
All of them work. What we are building is the combination: fund
production, reserve it, buy it, and see the farm through hardware. We
did not find that in one place. That is a hypothesis we have to prove,
not a claim."

## 1:55-2:15 Dokaz traznje (slajd 6)

"Before the marketplace, we sold our first product: the smart hive. We
asked for 140 thousand dollars. 150 people backed 300 hives in 27
hours, on Futard.
That money builds a real farm in Bosnia right now, and we film every
step.
This is proof people want to take part in real production. It is the
base the marketplace starts from. It is not the marketplace's traction.
That comes next."

(Preko glasa: snimci farme, ako su zavedeni.)

## 2:15-2:35 Trziste i sirenje (slajd 7)

"We start with honey because that is our farm and our people. We look
at four markets where we know beekeepers: Texas, the European Union,
Montreal and Brazil.
The numbers on the slide are category demand, bottom-up, from official
data. Texas alone is about 71 million dollars of honey a year at retail.
We have not set how much of that we can reach. We will, from the first
sales.
Then the same model goes to other farms: cattle, milk, other things
that produce. That part is the vision, not a product yet."

## 2:35-2:50 Sledeci korak i tim (slajd 8)

"What we build next: the main user journeys, the first network of
beekeepers we know, every listing linked to the farm, sales tests in
the four markets, and onboarding we can repeat.
We earn 5 percent on each sale. Our milestone is simple: people buying
again from the same beekeeper, in more than one market.
I am a beekeeper and an engineer and I run an apitherapy centre. Sinisa
manages the farm. We were at Colosseum twice with the smart hive. This
time we bring the platform. Thank you."
