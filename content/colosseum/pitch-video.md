# Pitch video - scenario v2 (2026-09-24)

INTERNO. Prati `deck.md` v2 i istih sest Pixar koraka. Placeholderi su isti
kao u decku; izgovaraju se samo kad ih Nemanja potvrdi.

Duzina: **2 do 3 minuta** (colosseum.com/hackathon, "prezentacioni video
2-3 minuta", hackathon.md). Ovaj scenario je oko 2:40 kad se govori
normalnim tempom. Ako je preko 3:00, prvo se skracuje korak 6, pa korak 2.
Frontier pitch (https://youtu.be/denQhkGyNjs) je bio o farmi; ovaj ne sme
da zvuci kao on.

Format: Nemanja u kadru, pa slajdovi ili ekran aplikacije. Snimak sa farme
ili kosnice ide preko glasa samo ako postoji na Drive-u (zavesti u
materials/index.md). Demo aplikacije je poseban video (`demo-video.md`);
ovde ide najvise 10 sekundi ekrana, kao dokaz da postoji.

---

## 0:00-0:15 Cover: ko smo (slajd 1)

"Hi, I am Nemanja, and we are HiveBits.
HiveBits is the global farmers' market for honey: you know the beekeeper
behind every jar, and he gets almost three times more for it.
This month we closed our pre-seed on MetaDAO, goal in 27 hours,
oversubscribed. That money is building our own smart bee farm right now.
We are beekeepers. Here is what we build on top of that."

Kadar: Nemanja u kameru. Na ekranu recenica kategorije i "Pre-seed raised
on MetaDAO in 27 hours". Kriterijum 1 (founder fit) i 5 (komunikacija)
moraju da se vide u ovih 15 sekundi.

## 0:15-0:40 Korak 1, Once upon a time: problem (slajd 2)

"Have you heard the term health-safe food?
It is food where you can check where it comes from, and what is inside.
People buy honey because they want something natural. And they buy it
from a label in a supermarket. In the US, 46 million households buy
honey. Only 3% of it online.
The EU tested imported honey at the border. Almost half of the samples
were suspected of adulteration. Suspected, not proven.
So the buyer pays for natural, and he does not know what he eats."

Na ekranu: "46% suspected" i "3% online", izvor sitno.

## 0:40-1:05 Korak 2, And every day: zasto danas ne radi (slajd 3)

"How do people solve this today?
They trust the label. Nobody can check a label.
They buy from a beekeeper they know. That works only if you live near him.
The real proof is a lab test, and that is for importers, not for one jar.
And the beekeeper? In the US he gets 2 dollars 45 a pound from a packer.
When he sells it himself, 7 dollars 15. Almost three times more. But he
cannot reach the buyers, so most of his honey goes to the packer.
I know this, because I am that beekeeper."

Na ekranu: "$2.45 vs $7.15 a pound. Almost 3x. USDA, 2025."
`[Nemanjina brojka iz prakse ako je ima, umesto ili uz USDA]`

## 1:05-1:30 Korak 3, Until one day: resenje (slajd 4)

"So we build HiveBits. A marketplace for bee products. Only that, for now.
We verify the beekeeper. He lists a batch.
The batch gets a record on Solana: who, where, which harvest.
The buyer scans the jar and sees it before he buys.
When the jar sells, the beekeeper gets USDC. Same moment.
Our own farm is supplier number one. Nobody has a live marketplace for
bee products on a blockchain today. We are building the first one."

Kadar: do 10 sekundi ekrana aplikacije `[Nemanja potvrdjuje sta postoji]`.
Ako demo nije gotov, ide slajd, ne izmisljen ekran.

## 1:30-1:50 Korak 4, And because of that: zasto bolje (slajd 5)

"Point by point.
Trust: not a label, a record for that batch. On our own farm, hive data
too.
Reach: the good beekeeper is not only local any more.
Proof: no lab. You scan the jar.
Price: the beekeeper sells at his direct price. We take 5%. He keeps the
rest. That is the difference between 2 dollars and 7."

## 1:50-2:15 Korak 5, And because of that: traction (slajd 6)

"What is proven. The company first: 140 thousand dollars pre-seed on
MetaDAO, 27 hours, oversubscribed. Before that, 200 thousand dollars from
our hives, and we sell out every season. The pre-seed builds our farm,
300 hives, supplier number one.
The platform: beekeepers in Brazil, Texas, Montreal and Croatia for the
start. [Prvi pcelari koji su rekli da, prva serija, prva prodaja.]
We were on Colosseum twice before, both times with the farm. The farm is
funded. The marketplace is what we built during this hackathon."

`[brojke platforme]` samo ako ih Nemanja potvrdi. Bez njih se recenica
preskace, ne popunjava.

## 2:15-2:35 Korak 6, Until finally: trziste (slajd 7)

"Start with the US. 46 million households buy honey, 18 dollars a year.
That is 839 million dollars. Our 5% is 42 million a year, from honey, in
one country. Europe imports 175 thousand tonnes a year from outside the
EU, and that is where the fraud suspicion sits.
Then the same system for other foods with the same problem. Then B2B,
shops and importers, the AgriDex model, on Solana."

## 2:35-2:50 Posle koraka: Solana, ask (slajd 10 -> 12)

"Why Solana: the buyer scans a jar and sees the beekeeper, no wallet
needed to look. The beekeeper in Brazil gets USDC the moment the jar
sells. And a record for every jar costs almost nothing.
We want to win this and join the Colosseum accelerator.
I am Nemanja, this is HiveBits. Know what you eat."

Napomene za snimanje
- Nemanja govori svojim recima; tekst je okvir, ne tekst za citanje.
- Nikad "fake honey" u izgovoru. Uvek "suspected".
- Bez tvrdnji o lekovitosti meda.
- Ne pominjati lokaciju farme ni rokove izgradnje.
- "Pre-seed", ne "ICO".
- Isti snimci sa terena (zemljiste, kontejneri, kosnice) sluze i za
  nedeljni jednominutni update na Colosseum-u (preporucen, hackathon.md).
