# Pitch video - scenario v1 (2026-09-22)

INTERNO. Prati `deck.md` v1 i istih sest Pixar koraka. Placeholderi su isti
kao u decku; izgovaraju se samo kad ih Nemanja potvrdi.

Ciljna duzina: 3:00. `[max duzina pitch videa - proveriti na colosseum.com
kad objave pravila]`. Frontier pitch (https://youtu.be/denQhkGyNjs) je bio o
farmi; ovaj ne sme da zvuci kao on.

Format: Nemanja u kadru, pa slajdovi. Snimak sa farme ili kosnice ide preko
glasa samo ako postoji na Drive-u (zavesti u materials/index.md).

---

## 0:00-0:25 Korak 1, Once upon a time: problem (slajd 1 -> 2)

"Have you heard the term health-safe food?
It is food where you can check where it comes from, and what is inside.
People buy honey because they want something natural and safe.
But the EU tested imported honey at the border, and almost half of the
samples were suspected to be adulterated. Suspected, not proven.
When you buy a jar, you do not know what you eat."

Kadar: Nemanja direktno u kameru. Na ekranu "46% suspected" + izvor sitno.

## 0:25-0:55 Korak 2, And every day: zasto danas ne radi (slajd 3)

"How do people solve this today?
They trust the label. But nobody can check a label.
They buy from a beekeeper they know. That works only if you live near him.
The real proof is a lab test, and that is for importers, not for one jar.
And the good beekeeper sells in bulk. The brand keeps the value.
I know this, because I am that beekeeper."

## 0:55-1:20 Korak 3, Until one day: resenje (slajd 4)

"So we build HiveBits. A marketplace for bee products. Only that, for now.
We verify the beekeeper. He lists his honey.
Every batch gets a record on Solana: who, where, which harvest.
The buyer sees it before he buys."

Kadar: `[demo ekran - Nemanja potvrdjuje sta postoji]`. Ako demo nije gotov,
ide slajd, ne izmisljen ekran.

## 1:20-1:50 Korak 4, And because of that: zasto bolje (slajd 5)

"Point by point.
Trust: not a label, a record for that batch.
Reach: the good beekeeper is not only local any more.
Proof: you do not need a lab, it comes with the jar.
And the beekeeper sells direct and gets paid in USDC, same day, in Brazil
or in Europe."

## 1:50-2:15 Korak 5, And because of that: traction (slajd 6)

"What is proven. Since Frontier we raised, and we are building our own farm.
That money goes to the farm, like we promised.
The farm is supplier number one of the marketplace.
We have beekeepers in Brazil, Texas, Montreal and the EU.
Before, we pitched the farm. The farm is the proof. The marketplace is the
company."

`[N beekeepers]` i `[revenue]` samo ako ih Nemanja potvrdi.

## 2:15-2:40 Korak 6, Until finally: trziste (slajd 7)

"In 2024 Americans ate almost 700 million pounds of honey, a record.
Europe imported more than 360 thousand tonnes in 2022.
Our math: buyers, times what they spend, times our fee.
Then the same system for other foods. Then B2B, shops and importers.
AgriDex already shows B2B agri trade works on Solana."

## 2:40-3:00 Posle koraka: Solana, ask (slajd 10 -> 12)

"Why Solana: a beekeeper anywhere gets paid the same day, and a record for
every jar costs almost nothing.
We are looking for [ask]. If you know a beekeeper, a shop or an importer
who wants to prove their food is real, talk to us.
I am Nemanja, this is HiveBits. Know what you eat."

Napomene za snimanje
- Nemanja govori svojim recima; tekst je okvir, ne tekst za citanje.
- Nikad "fake honey" u izgovoru. Uvek "suspected".
- Bez tvrdnji o lekovitosti meda.
- Ne pominjati lokaciju farme ni rokove izgradnje.
