# Pitch video - scenario v9 (2026-10-11)

INTERNO. Prati `deck.md` v9 (sedam slajdova po Nemanjinom brief-u od
11.10). Ograde iz beleski se ne izgovaraju.

Duzina: **2 do 3 minuta** (colosseum.com/hackathon, hackathon.md). Ovaj
scenario je oko 2:20 normalnim tempom, sa rezervom do 3:00 za pauze i
kadrove sa terena. Ako treba duze, prvo se prosiruje slajd 5 (sta se
gradi od rejza, uz snimke) i slajd 3 (demo kupovine), ne slajd 6.

Pixar tok: problem (2) -> postojece ogranicenje (2, 3) -> resenje (3) ->
vrednost i kako raste (4) -> dokaz traznje (5) -> rast (6) -> sledeci
korak (7).

Format: Nemanja u kadru, pa slajdovi. Kadrovi sa farme (kosnice, vosak,
Sinisa) preko glasa samo ako postoje na Drive-u i zavedeni su u
`materials/index.md`. Demo je poseban video (`demo-video.md`).

Jezik: prosto i direktno, kako Nemanja govori. Bez em dash. Bez rokova,
mesta farme, iznosa i obecanja.

---

## 0:00-0:15 Cover (slajd 1)

"Hi, I am Nemanja. We are HiveBits.
HiveBits connects buyers directly with bee products from beekeepers
they can trust.
We start with beekeepers we know. Later, our hardware brings more
beekeepers into the network."

## 0:15-0:35 Problem (slajd 2)

"When you buy honey, you usually see a label. You do not see the
beekeeper.
We think trust starts with the person who makes the honey.
So we put the beekeeper and where the honey comes from in front, not
just the product."

## 0:35-0:55 Resenje (slajd 3)

"The marketplace is simple. You find a beekeeper. You see what he has.
You buy directly from him.
That is it. No extra steps."

(Ovde moze kratak kadar iz demo videa ako interfejs postoji, #47.)

## 0:55-1:20 Kako raste mreza (slajd 4)

"We do not need every beekeeper on day one. We start with people we
know and work with.
Our hardware gives us a way to bring in new beekeepers later, with
monitoring data from their hives.
The hardware does not replace trust. It helps us keep the connection to
real production."

## 1:20-1:45 Dokaz traznje (slajd 5)

"Before the marketplace we sold our first product, the smart hive.
150 people backed 300 smart hives in 27 hours.
That money builds the farm now, and we record every step.
This farm and these beekeepers are the base for the marketplace."

(Preko glasa: snimci farme i kosnica sa Drive-a, ako su zavedeni.)

## 1:45-2:05 Trzista (slajd 6)

"We start with honey. We look at four markets where we have beekeepers
we know: Brazil, Texas, Montreal and the European Union.
We will test demand, supply and logistics there.
We take 5 percent of each sale. The beekeeper chooses if he pays it or
adds it to his price."

## 2:05-2:20 Sledeci korak i tim (slajd 7)

"Our next milestone is repeat sales. Not one sale, but people who come
back to the same beekeeper.
For that we build the marketplace, onboard the first beekeepers,
connect each product to where it comes from, and test sales in the four
markets.
I am a beekeeper and an engineer, and I run an apitherapy centre. Sinisa
manages the farm.
Thank you."

Provera: svaka brojka u govoru (150, 300, 27 sati, 5%) ima izvor u
`deck.md` na istom slajdu. Marketplace se govori u sadasnjem vremenu po
Nemanjinoj instrukciji iz brief-a; ako interfejs ne postoji do snimanja,
slajd 3 prelazi u "we are building" (#47). Nema rokova, lokacije farme,
iznosa, TAM brojke ni ask-a.
