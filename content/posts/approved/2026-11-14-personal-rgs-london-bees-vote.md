I am in London, in front of the Royal Geographical Society.

In 2008 there was a debate in this building. Five scientists, five species: fungi, bats, plankton, primates and bees. The question was which one we cannot live without.

The audience voted for bees.

I work with bees every day. Good to see that people here agree.
