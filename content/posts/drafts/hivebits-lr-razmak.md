## POST (~235 znakova)

The LR hive is built around one thing bees do. They always leave a small space between the combs so they can walk. Langstroth made frames that keep that space. So we can take one frame out and put it back without breaking the comb.

## STA DA SNIMIS (oko 30 sekundi)

Prvi kadar je ram koji izlazi iz kosnice, ne pozdrav.

1. **Ruke vade ram (5s).** "This is one frame from the hive."
2. **Krupno razmak izmedju dva rama (8-10s).** "Look at the space between
   the frames. Bees always leave this space so they can walk."
3. **Ram u ruci, pcele na njemu (8-10s).** "If the space is bigger, they
   build comb in it. If it is smaller, they close it."
4. **Vracas ram nazad (5s).** "So we can take the frame out and put it back
   and nothing is broken."
