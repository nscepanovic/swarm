## Sekundarni postovi: pcele

Spremni, idu kao drugi post u danu ili kad glavnog nema.

**Zima, lopta oko matice.** Potvrditi poslednju recenicu, ako uredjaj ne meri
temperaturu na nasim kosnicama, ona ide napolje.

In winter the bees do not sleep. They make a ball around the queen and shake their wing muscles to make heat. The bees outside slowly move inside, so nobody freezes. We track the temperature inside the hive, so we see this without opening it.

**Trutovi.**

This time of year the bees throw the drones out of the hive. Drones are the male bees. They do not collect food, and the colony will not feed them through the winter. In spring the colony makes new ones.

**Zimske pcele.**

A bee born in summer lives only a few weeks. She works until she is worn out. A bee born in autumn lives for months. She has one job, to keep the colony alive until spring. The bees in our hives now are winter bees.
